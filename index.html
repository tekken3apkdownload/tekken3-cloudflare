So which one is actually right for you?

Simple rule: new-ish phone → standard build. Phone older than five years or nearly full → a light build. Hate black bars → full-screen. Want every fighter today → mod. And if a current build misbehaves on a genuinely old handset, drop to an older version rather than giving up on the game.

The last case deserves one extra sentence, because it is the one players get wrong: newer is not automatically smoother on old hardware. If your device falls in that bracket, read the Tekken 3 APK old version notes before choosing — they explain which earlier builds suit low-end phones and what you give up with each.

Whichever you pick, install works the same way

Download the file, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser under Settings → Security, open the file and tap Install. Every build carries its own emulator, needs no account, and plays fully offline on Android 4.4 and above. Swapping builds later is painless too — uninstalling and installing a different variant takes a minute, and your decision today is not permanent.
